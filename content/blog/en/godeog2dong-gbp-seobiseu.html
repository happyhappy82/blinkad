What the Profile Can Help Customers Check

Business Profile is the current name of the service formerly called Google My Business. Eligible businesses can manage information on Google Search and Maps without a Google profile subscription charge. Features and public presentation can vary; a profile is not a guaranteed position at the top or on a particular part of the search screen.

The source described 893 businesses as of January 20, 2026, including 805 classified as local businesses, or 90.1%. This edition retains that stated date, but the underlying register and classification method were not available for independent reconstruction. These are not a fresh count of currently trading businesses.

A local-business share does not establish how likely residents are to use a neighborhood phrase or Google. The practical question is whether people who do find the business can obtain reliable contact, service and access information.

The original also connected a detailed profile with AI answers. AEO describes efforts to make answers useful and understandable; it is not the name of a guaranteed Google placement. Accurate business information helps explain the real business, but this edition has not measured whether a profile field increases AI selection. Google’s website guidance says there is no special markup that ensures inclusion in its AI search features.

Give Customers the Information They Need

1. Understand the Question Behind the Search

The following examples translate the original ideas. They are prompts for content planning, not newly observed popular search terms.

  • Start with the actual category. The source listed 70 Korean-food businesses (7.8%) and 76 real-estate services (8.5%). A restaurant might need to explain lunch or other actual menu choices; a property agency needs different service and contact details. Counts alone do not measure current demand or Google competition.
  • Use a location that genuinely applies. The source’s street shares were Godeok-ro 28.2%, Godeok Biz Valley-ro 23.7%, and Godeok-ro 83-gil 10.9%. Building labels included Godeok Gracium Apartments with 149 businesses, “Gangdong” with 100, and Godeok Central Prugio with 69. These are source references, not verified current tenant counts; “Gangdong” does not identify a precise building by itself. Check the actual place before using a landmark.

Different intentions call for different answers:

  • Comparing options: parking information or whether a restaurant can accommodate a family, where that is true.
  • Finding a particular business: a phone number or opening hours. The original Ilho Salon and Ewha Real Estate Agency examples are named source illustrations, not current client inspections.
  • Taking action: a supported booking, order or contact route for a service the business actually provides.

2. Keep the Important Profile Details Reliable

  • Name, address and phone: use the real-world name and actual location and contact details. Keep important facts consistent with the website and relevant channels, allowing for legitimate platform formatting and supported contact features.
  • Category: choose the most accurate available category, with relevant additional ones where needed. A more specific label is useful only when it is available and describes the actual main business.
  • Description: explain the actual service and relevant location within the supported field. Do not add unrelated keywords or sales offers. The original example of a ground-floor restaurant using organic ingredients should be used only if the location and ingredient claim can be supported.
  • Photos and videos: show the actual entrance, interior and representative products or services. Use appropriate permission for identifiable people; do not stage an image as proof of an experience or result that did not occur.
  • Hours: keep normal and special opening times current, including relevant temporary closures. Submit accurate changes promptly and check their review status.
  • Reviews: offer genuine customers an optional route for honest feedback without incentives, positive-only selection, pressure or requested content. Give relevant, factual replies and protect private information.
  • Posts: share real news, menu changes, offers or events where supported. Dates, terms and Google review matter. A steady posting rhythm is a work choice, not a published guarantee that Google will favor the profile.

4. Review What Customers Can Actually Use

  • Consistency: check the important facts across the profile, website and social channels when a service, contact detail or schedule changes.
  • Mobile use: inspect the actual public information and linked contact or booking page on a phone. The business can improve its content and website, while Google controls the profile interface.
  • Genuine feedback: do not buy reviews or organize misleading contributions. A request should allow customers to express their own experience, including a negative one.
  • Relevant local language: mention Godeok 2-dong, Godeok Gracium or Godeok Station only where useful and accurate. Do not append landmarks to the business name merely for search coverage.
  • Performance: review the current Performance view and the measures actually available to the profile. Use matching periods and definitions. Views, call clicks, direction requests and website clicks are different from completed inquiries, arrivals or sales, and Google’s report can include advertising activity.

Update the Details When the Business Changes

Update the profile when important information changes and use regular checks to find omissions. A useful routine should help a customer reach the right service and place; its commercial effect still needs to be assessed against actual outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create or claim a Business Profile?

Use the appropriate Google account, check for the correct existing profile and follow the add-or-claim process for an eligible business. Complete the verification options Google offers. Mail is not the universal default: Google determines the available methods, which can include recorded or live video, phone, email or mail, and further review may be required.

Can a business in a larger category still benefit?

It can make the actual service and visitor information clearer. Use representative photos, accurate explanations and helpful review handling. A larger category count does not determine every search result, and neither regular posts nor a fuller description guarantees a ranking or more sales.

How often should I update the information?

Act when hours, services, access or offers genuinely change, and set a manageable review schedule for the rest. Avoid unnecessary edits to correct information just to appear active. Some edits or posts require review, so distinguish submitting a change from it being publicly accepted.
