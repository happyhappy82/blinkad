At a glance
Before adding Google Ads to a business outside Korea, check where an ad click leads, what visitors can learn there and how they can get in touch. If the map information is wrong, there is no page in the customer's language or the booking button fails, advertising simply brings more people to that frustrating experience. Define the audience and language, then prepare the Maps profile, landing page, contact route and conversion tracking so you can see where spending is being wasted.

When sales fall or a business needs new customers, advertising is often the first option that comes to mind. Set a budget, choose some keywords, and it can feel as though customers should start arriving.

But advertising cannot fill in missing information. Running ads while Maps shows the wrong opening hours, the website is available only in Korean or the booking button does not work may produce clicks without inquiries. Before spending, open the page a customer will see after the ad.

Describe the customer and the offer in one sentence

Treating “all non-Korean customers in the area” as one audience makes both the ad and its destination vague. Tourists and local residents may be looking for different services, visiting at different times and needing different explanations.

Before writing an ad, complete this sentence:

Which customer, in which area and situation, would book or buy what from our business?

A professional in Singapore booking a Korean-style hair service after work and a Korean newcomer to Toronto looking for a nearby dentist need different approaches. Language is only part of the difference. Their search terms, comparison criteria and questions also change.

Bring Google Maps information into line with the actual business

For a physical location, review Google Business Profile before the ads. The address, opening hours, phone number, business category, photographs and website should reflect how the business operates now.

Google Ads location assets can show information such as the business address, a map, distance and a call button. Connecting Google Business Profile to the advertising account lets you use its listed business locations as location assets. Google Ads: location assets

Outdated profile details can appear alongside advertising. Check the connected branches, phone numbers and opening hours as well as the ad account itself.

The landing page should continue the promise made in the ad

If an ad says English consultations are available, customers should be able to read about the service and booking process in English after clicking. If the ad promotes a particular menu item or treatment, send visitors to a page explaining it rather than the general homepage.

Google Ads provides landing-page reports to help review mobile usability and performance after a click. Google Ads: landing-page performance Before launching, open the page on an actual phone and check:

  • Does it load properly on a phone used in the local market?
  • Does the first screen show the service described in the ad?
  • Is the information needed to decide, such as pricing or consultation conditions, available?
  • Are the call, messaging and booking buttons easy to tap?
  • Do links stay with the right branch and language?

Have the person handling inquiries test the whole process

A visible button does not prove an inquiry will reach anyone. A phone number might lack a country code, a WhatsApp link might point to the wrong number, or a submitted booking form might send no notification.

Before switching on ads, choose a customer scenario and send a test inquiry. Enter a name and contact details, submit it, then check whether the responsible person receives a notification, can identify the inquiry language and knows who will reply.

More contact options are not always helpful. Keep one or two channels the business can monitor, and state operating hours and expected response times. This can also reduce duplicate messages from customers who send the same question elsewhere while waiting.

Measure real inquiries rather than treating every click as a conversion

Judging advertising by clicks alone can make a campaign look successful even when it produces no inquiries. First decide which actions matter to the business, then check that they are being recorded.

Google Ads supports website conversion actions such as reaching a particular page, submitting a form or clicking a button. Its guidance also explains measurement using the Google tag or Google Tag Manager. Google Ads: website conversion setup

You do not need a long list of metrics. Start with actions that matter to the business, and record each separately:

If conversions in the advertising dashboard do not match actual bookings, review the setup first. An automatically recorded action should not simply be read as revenue.

Answer five questions before launching

You can get a useful first picture of readiness without a complicated audit. Can you answer all five?

  • Can you describe the intended customer and the product or service in one sentence?
  • Do the address, hours and phone number on Google Maps match current information?
  • Does the ad open a page in the same language about the same service?
  • Does a test inquiry or booking reach the responsible person?
  • Can you record inquiries and bookings separately from clicks?

If any answer is unclear, address that issue before increasing the budget. Once the preparations are complete, compare spend, clicks, inquiries and bookings over the same period to find where customers are being lost.

Frequently asked questions

Can we start Google Ads without a website?

Some ad formats can run without a website, but customers still need somewhere to check the service and its conditions. If you rely on Google Business Profile alone, first check that the address, hours, phone number, photographs and contact route reflect current operations.

How many languages does the landing page need?

You do not need every language at the outset. Start with the language of customers already making inquiries or the audience chosen for advertising. Make sure they can follow both the service explanation and the booking process in that language.

Can we begin by watching clicks without conversion tracking?

A click shows interest in the ad, not a completed inquiry or booking. Before launching, prepare to measure at least one action close to the business, such as a form submission, call-button click or completed booking.
