A Practical Registration and Management Checklist

  • Business details: use the real-world name, precise address, phone, website where available, and current hours. Include Yangjae-daero only when it belongs to the actual address. Review holiday and temporary changes before customers rely on an old schedule.
  • Categories: select the most accurate available primary category, adding other categories only where relevant. Do not list a clinic as offering a specialty it does not provide, or add a category simply to cover a search phrase.
  • Photos and videos: show the actual exterior, interior and representative products or service setting. The original five-photo minimum is a starter suggestion, not a Google ranking threshold. Obtain appropriate permission for identifiable staff and customers; patient privacy requires particular care.
  • Reviews: the source proposed responding within 48 hours. Treat that as an agreed work target, subject to factual checks and approvals, rather than a Google deadline. Offer genuine customers an optional feedback route without rewards, positive-only selection, pressure or requested wording, and keep private details out of replies.
  • Posts: share useful news, events or offers where supported, using actual conditions and dates. A weekly post was the original schedule suggestion; publication needs review and the schedule does not ensure ranking or repeat visits.
  • Attributes: select available details such as Wi-Fi, parking, takeaway or accessibility only where true. Do not infer a step-free entrance or accessible route merely because the building has a lift.
