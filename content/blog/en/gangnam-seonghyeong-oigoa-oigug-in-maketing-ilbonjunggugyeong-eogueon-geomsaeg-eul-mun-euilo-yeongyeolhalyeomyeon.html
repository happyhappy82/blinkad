Changing the search language does not always show the same clinics

Gangnam's official medical tourism figures reported 650,958 international patients in 2025. Plastic surgery represented 22.7% of the specialty breakdown. By nationality, Japan accounted for 37.9%, China 31.8% and the United States 8%. As of May 1, 2026, 44 of the 148 medical institutions in Gangnam's medical tourism partner program were listed in the plastic surgery category.

These figures are not an individual clinic's patient count or booking results. They provide context for why international patients in Gangnam cannot all be treated as one English-speaking audience.

Separate shared medical facts from language-specific instructions

English, Japanese and Chinese pages need both facts that stay the same and instructions adapted to how the audience will use them.

Information that should remain consistent across languages

  • The official medical institution name, address and hours
  • Current doctors' names, areas of practice and verifiable qualifications
  • The operations and treatments currently provided
  • An explanation that suitability is assessed through consultation
  • Medical team-reviewed recovery guidance, precautions and possible side effects
  • Conditions and inclusions if prices are published
  • The distinction between an inquiry received and a confirmed booking
  • Checks relating to international patient recruitment registration and medical advertising

Information to decide separately for each language

  • The procedure terms patients actually search for
  • Inquiry channels staffed in that language
  • Response hours in Korea Standard Time and the expected reply time
  • Materials to prepare from overseas and checks before visiting
  • The distinction between Simplified and Traditional Chinese
  • The confirmation message shown after an inquiry is submitted

In this five-site review, English pages used WhatsApp or email, Japanese pages used LINE, and Chinese pages used WeChat, online inquiries or region-specific LINE contacts. That does not mean nationality alone should determine the messenger. Check both where actual inquiries come from and which channels your team can operate.

Decide whether to maintain one Chinese version or separate Simplified and Traditional versions according to the regions you serve. Google Search Central supports zh-Hans for Simplified Chinese and zh-Hant for Traditional Chinese. If both versions are maintained, separate URLs and hreflang links between corresponding pages provide a clear arrangement.

Avoid separating URLs while leaving Korean text in the content, or updating English medical team details while Japanese pages retain old information. After translation, check medical team details, services, hours and button links against the reference page as well as reviewing grammar.

Test one inquiry from start to finish before expanding all three languages

Before increasing the page count, check whether an inquiry in one language works all the way through:

  1. Agree one reference sheet for shared facts.
    Keep current clinic and doctor names, service scope, address, hours, price conditions and precautions in one place.

  2. Choose a priority language and service.
    Consider current inquiry records, staffing and care the clinic actually provides, rather than following nationality totals alone. Complete one main procedure page for each of English, Japanese and Chinese.

  3. Connect the actual inquiry channel for each language.
    Check that the app opens, someone with an overseas number can send a message and the responsible team will answer. State supported languages and operating hours in Korea Standard Time.

  4. Test the full mobile process.
    Follow Google Maps search → website → language selection → surgical information → inquiry sent → automatic guidance → staff reply. Do not use automatic replies that confirm clinical suitability or outcomes.

  5. Record traffic and inquiry status separately by language.
    Use UTMs on Maps, ad and language-page links, and distinguish visits, contact-button clicks and form submissions. Manage staff replies and confirmed bookings separately in the approved inquiry or booking system.

Do not send personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses or phone numbers to Google Analytics. Keep medical inquiry details connected to a person, such as treatment concerns, surgical history or medication, out of analytics events and URLs too. Use non-identifying actions such as ja_line_click and zh_consult_submit in analytics, while keeping conversation content in a system with managed access.

Before undertaking international patient recruitment, check the medical institution's registration status and assess whether language-specific pages and ads require prior medical advertising review for their medium and content. Translation can make a claim stronger than the Korean original. Recheck exaggerated or guaranteed claims and material needing medical team confirmation before publication.

Frequently asked questions

Can we create English, Japanese and Chinese pages with machine translation first?

It can help with an initial draft, but we do not recommend publishing that draft unchanged. Review facts that must stay accurate, including medical team details, service scope and precautions, alongside procedure terminology, contact buttons and hours in each language. Do not publish a language your inquiry team cannot actually support.

Must Simplified and Traditional Chinese always be separate?

Separating them is clearer if you target different regions, such as mainland China and Taiwan or Hong Kong, and can actually respond to both audiences. Initially, you can prioritize the version with existing inquiries and available staff. Make that scope clear rather than implying support for all Chinese-speaking regions.

How can we tell which language generated an inquiry?

Use distinguishable UTMs and non-identifying interaction events on language pages and contact buttons, and record the initial language and source separately in the inquiry system. Keep visits and clicks, submitted inquiries, staff replies and confirmed bookings separate to see which language's process is working.
