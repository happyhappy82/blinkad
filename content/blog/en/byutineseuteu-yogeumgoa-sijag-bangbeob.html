BeautyNest pricing has two parts: a monthly subscription and usage charges. There is no installation fee. The subscription covers access to the tool, while usage depends on conversations that actually take place. The amount varies with business size and connected channels, so we provide it in a consultation. This article explains how the charges are structured and how to get started.

Why separate the subscription from usage?

The monthly subscription covers the unified screen, automatic translation, automatic replies and return-visit reminder settings. It stays the same whether inquiry volume is high or low.

Usage charges apply when an inquiry becomes a conversation. A message that arrives without any exchange is not chargeable under this rule.

Beauty businesses can have large seasonal and weekday fluctuations. Inquiries may concentrate around holidays or fall to half the level in a quieter period. A fixed fee alone can place a greater burden on the business during quieter months.

How to get started

1. Count current inquiries

Prepare the following four figures before requesting a quote:

  • Total monthly inquiries across all channels
  • How many became actual conversations
  • The number of touch-up or session reminders sent
  • The number of staff providing services

Without figures, estimates have to rely on business size and may differ from actual use.

2. Choose the channels to connect

You do not need every channel at once. Start with the one or two receiving the most inquiries, then expand after the team is comfortable using the system.

3. Prepare the messages

Have answers to five common questions ready, along with the recommended return intervals for each service. Having those details ready helps determine how quickly onboarding can proceed.

4. Adjust through use

Use the first one or two weeks to refine the timing and wording of automatic messages.

How to judge the cost

The comparison matters as much as the amount. Consider:

  • Appointments missed because staff could not check inquiries while serving a customer
  • The cost of an additional front-desk team member
  • The advertising cost of generating the same number of new inquiries

If you have never counted missed inquiries, start there. Check how many arrive while a service runs longer than expected. That gives you practical numbers for the decision.

Questions to ask with the quote

Clarify these items to avoid unexpected charges:

  • Is there a per-person charge when several stylists or therapists use the system?
  • What unit is used to measure usage?
  • Can the business check billed usage against its own records?
  • Are touch-up or session reminders charged separately?
  • What are the minimum term and early-termination conditions?
  • Can customer history be exported later?

The final question is especially important if you later move to another system. Customer records are a business asset; without an export, moving may require starting again.

Frequently asked questions

Is there really no installation fee?

There is none. Connecting channels and setting up messages are included in onboarding.

Will a quieter month cost less?

The usage portion falls. The subscription remains the same.

How are multiple branches priced?

We consider the number of branches and team members. The setup also depends on whether locations need separate or combined views, so explain that during the consultation.

What if it is not a good fit after trying it?

BeautyNest does not change your booking or payment software, so stopping its use leaves those existing tools in place. Confirm the contract conditions during consultation.

Is pricing the same as DoctorNest?

The calculation uses the same framework. Actual amounts are quoted separately because beauty businesses have different inquiry fluctuations and team structures.

Is a PC required?

The current offering is PC-based. Check beforehand whether inquiries are handled at a desk.
