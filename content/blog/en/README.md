# Reviewed English insights

Only add an entry to index.json after completing the corresponding English article and its meaning/copy review. Each entry uses the Korean article's existing id; its body is stored in <id>.html.

For each article:
1. Read and snapshot the current published Korean source, including its publication date and source hash.
2. Write the full English version for the same business audience. Preserve conditions, claims, examples, tables and sources.
3. Compare the finished English article with the Korean meaning, then review English grammar, idiom, terminology and paragraph flow separately. Record who performed the review; do not imply native-speaker or independent review if none occurred.
4. Label links to untranslated Korean articles as Korean. Use English site navigation and the English contact page.
5. Register the reviewed entry in index.json. publishedAt is the real English publication date; originalPublishedAt is the Korean date.
6. Check the English list/detail, matching Korean original, canonical, reciprocal language links, metadata, sitemap and unknown untranslated URLs before publishing.

Drafts and review records belong in the private working records. The Korean post data and Notion schedule remain unchanged. English bodies are authored files, not browser translations or fallbacks to Korean.
