A proposal should show what will actually change

Updating Google Maps information, building pages in another language and managing content over time involve different work. When comparing international marketing proposals, look for three things.

First, identify what will change. Separate the profiles to manage, languages and pages to create, and ads and content to run. If website changes are needed, clarify what must be requested from your current web agency.

Second, agree how completed work will be verified. Use evidence suited to the task: before-and-after screens for information updates, live URLs and working contact links for new pages, and final copy and publication URLs for content. For AI search reviews, also ask for the questions, languages, dates and actual responses.

Third, clarify support after publication. Discuss separately whether changes to medical team information or opening hours, ongoing ad management and additional content are included. Our guide to hospital website costs and project scope covers initial production and ongoing costs.

Use these points to agree the scope you need, then check that the selected work is reflected in the contract.

Provide official information and tell us how you can respond

Also confirm who manages the website, Google Maps profile and advertising account. Once the required work has been identified, we can clarify the access and cooperation needed for it.

Review visibility and actual inquiries separately

Completed work and customer response need separate checks. Updating a Google Maps profile or publishing a page is one result; how prospective patients respond is another. An increase in search visibility will not necessarily produce the same increase in inquiries.

If new inquiries are your priority, first agree which contact actions to track on which pages and ads. Distinguishing contact-button clicks, inquiries actually received and completed bookings helps identify what to improve next. We can also discuss how far the reporting can be connected with your hospital's own inquiry records.
