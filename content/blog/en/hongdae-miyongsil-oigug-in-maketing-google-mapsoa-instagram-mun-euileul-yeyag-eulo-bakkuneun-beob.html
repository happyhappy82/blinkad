The tool's name matters less than whether someone can find the necessary information through the whole process. Even with plenty of result photos on Instagram, missing price ranges or duration can send someone back to DMs. A large booking menu with unexplained differences makes the choice harder too.

Instagram can help people assess a style and start a conversation with photographs. If bookings stay only in DMs, dates, services, stylists and deposits can become scattered across messages. Keep the final details on a booking page or system managed by the salon.

If your Google Business Profile supports Instagram links, connect the correct account in its social profiles as well. Google says this feature is available only in selected regions and profiles, and does not supply separate social-link click metrics. Check availability in the profile editor.

Collect inquiry details once so the booking can move forward

Before a long Instagram discussion, collect the minimum booking information in a consistent order:

  • The desired service, or an indication that the person has not decided
  • The preferred date and available times
  • Photographs showing current hair condition and the desired result
  • A preferred stylist or No preference
  • The language in which they want a reply

Do not confirm final service suitability or an exact price from photographs alone. For items affected by bleaching history, hair length and condition or additional services, state conditions such as Starting at or Confirmed after consultation. Give the standard duration together with factors that may change it.

A staff reply should include the next step. Instead of ending at “this style needs a consultation,” explain which photographs and details are needed and send the branch's booking link. Explain which notification confirms the appointment after service and time selection. An inquiry received, a booking requested and a booking confirmed are different statuses.

Stylist profiles should use actual names, current services and work photographs approved for publication. Before describing a stylist as available in another language, confirm the language, level and available hours. Using a translation tool is different from offering regular consultations in that language.

Use a month to test the whole booking process

Maps views alone are not enough to assess international marketing for a Hongdae salon. Recording each step from search to confirmation helps identify where the process stops.

Week 1: check the Google Business Profile. Confirm the name matches the actual sign, along with the address, pin, hours, current branch photographs and services. The booking link should let someone book that branch, rather than opening a general page for several locations.

Week 2: check the booking page on mobile. Follow the English process through service selection, price conditions, duration, stylist, date, customer details, cancellation and deposit guidance, and confirmation. If several providers are connected, decide which link staff will recommend first.

Week 3: agree the order of Instagram replies. Prepare brief answers to common questions and a list of needed photographs and details. End replies with the same official booking link. Confirm that appointments agreed in DMs also appear in the final booking record.

Frequently asked questions

Can we manage bookings only through Instagram DMs?

It may work when volume is low and one person manages everything. However, dates, services, stylists, deposits and confirmation can be scattered through conversations. Use DMs for discussion and reference photographs, and keep final bookings in one system to help reduce omissions and duplication.

How should we show prices when they vary by customer?

List the base or starting price with the conditions that change it, such as hair length, bleaching history and extra services. If photos are not enough to confirm the price, clearly state Confirmed after consultation. If payment is taken during booking, make clear whether it is a deposit or the full price.

Can we measure international customers' bookings separately?

Use language-specific booking links and UTMs, and a preferred-language field in the booking form, rather than guessing nationality. Record Maps views, Instagram inquiries, booking starts, confirmations and actual visits separately to see which step has improved.
