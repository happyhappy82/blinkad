Answer What a Visitor Needs Before Booking

Google Business Profile is free for eligible businesses that meet customers in person. Authorized owners can maintain information associated with Google Search and Maps. Local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, so accurate content does not reserve first position.

“Restaurant in Gujwa-eup,” “accommodation in Woljeong-ri” and “café in Gimnyeong” are English planning examples, not newly measured Korean queries. Someone comparing a stay needs different details from someone choosing lunch. The source’s 98.2% local-business share does not establish that visitors prefer hidden independent places or measure their search intentions.

Agree on a Routine You Can Maintain

  • Prepare representative photos: ten can be an initial planning target, not a Google ranking minimum. Show actual rooms, entrances or services and replace images that no longer represent what a visitor will find.
  • Share worthwhile news: one or two posts a week is an editorial suggestion, not a ranking condition. Check supported types and publication status. Hotel profiles must follow the hotel-post restrictions, including the ban on offer posts and discount links.
  • Use the right fields: explain actual services, products or menus through supported fields. A restaurant’s dishes, such as the source’s pork or stew examples, should not automatically be treated as eligible retail products. Confirm current offerings and conditions before publishing prices.
  • Check the available contact route: legacy Business Profile chat ended on July 31, 2024. Google’s current guide describes WhatsApp or text options for claimed, verified profiles in selected regions; availability for a particular Jeju business is not established here. Use supported options or a working website contact route. The former Q&A API ended on November 3, 2025, without establishing that every profile has identical question features.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does every Gujwa-eup business need a profile? Check eligibility and actual customer discovery. A free profile can maintain useful Search and Maps information, but the local-business share does not establish Google as every owner’s best channel.
  • Why give reviews ongoing attention? Genuine experiences can answer prospective customers’ questions and reveal concerns to investigate. Reply factually and privately where necessary; reviews or replies do not guarantee trust, bookings or sales.
  • How often should photos change? Update them when the menu, room, setting or service changes. Ten photos is a planning suggestion rather than a threshold, and update frequency alone does not establish a ranking bonus.
