Marketing a hotel to international tourists takes more than listing rooms on booking platforms. Start by making room photographs, bed configurations, location, breakfast, check-in and cancellation terms clear enough for guests to compare.

Agoda's property-photo guidance covers photographs of both the property and individual room types. Trip.com's partner guidance lists registration details including the English property name and address, actual photographs, breakfast and child policies. Using these official sources, we look at what to prepare on platforms, Google Maps and the hotel website.

Agoda's official photo guidance distinguishes property photographs from those for each room type. Showing one attractive room in a hotel introduction does not accurately explain every room a guest can choose.

Photographs help guests understand size, beds, bathrooms, views and shared facilities. Avoid images of an earlier room condition or excessive editing. Trip.com likewise asks for photographs accurately reflecting the property's actual appearance and condition.

Does a hotel-name search lead to consistent official information?

After finding a hotel on a booking platform, a guest may search Google for its name or location. Google's hotel Business Profile guidance explains registration and verification for managing information on Search and Maps. Start with the hotel name, address, phone number, website and correct pin. For the wider management process, see BlinkAd's Google Maps marketing guide.

If the platform, profile and official site disagree, guests must work out which details are current. Regularly check:

  • The official English hotel name and address
  • Check-in and checkout times
  • Room types and bed configurations
  • Breakfast and additional charges
  • Parking, luggage storage and reception arrangements
  • The official phone number and contact channels

Rates and availability can change before booking. Link to the current booking screen rather than asserting a fixed price in a blog post.

Google's hotel-profile guidance addresses the name, address, phone number or website and verification of management access. Choosing a room on a platform and finding the hotel on Maps therefore need separate checks.

Which conditions must match when comparing the same room?

Suppose you compare a hotel's “Deluxe Double” across two channels. One rate includes breakfast and free cancellation; the other excludes breakfast and is non-refundable. Write down operational details so they are available to the whole team, rather than held by one staff member. If late check-in is offered, confirm its actual hours, advance-contact requirement and contact channel with the responsible person. Record when staff replies and booking-page details were compared. What role should the official hotel website play?

The website can be a reference for details a platform cannot fully explain. Help international guests find the following in one place. Before development, review language and booking with the international-customer website checklist.

  • Room-specific photographs and standard occupancy
  • Location and routes from airports or stations
  • Check-in, checkout and reception hours
  • Actual services such as breakfast, parking and luggage storage
  • Current booking and contact methods
  • The official place to check change and cancellation conditions

Review language pages against what guests actually ask. Google's multilingual-site guidance covers separate URLs, language-switching links and hreflang. Alongside the technical setup, check that booking and contact continue in the chosen language.

How should hotel information support search and AI?

Start by publishing official facts in an organized form. Answer room, location, hours and booking-policy questions briefly and directly under relevant headings. Identify the writer and last check date.

For “Is late check-in available?”, state the applicable times, whether advance contact is needed and where to confirm it. If hours may vary, give a pre-booking contact method.

Google's official AI search guidance explains that no special markup is required; ordinary Search technical requirements and helpful, reliable content still matter. To be eligible as a supporting link in Google's AI search features, a page must be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet.

How can platform and direct bookings work together?

Platforms can introduce the hotel to new customers; the official site can provide accurate information and support a direct relationship. Give each channel a clear role in the customer's decision.

Direct booking requires reliable management of current inventory, payment, cancellations, personal information and replies. Connecting only an inquiry form without these preparations may make confirmation take longer.

Separate platform bookings, official-site bookings or inquiries, Maps website clicks and direction requests. In Google's performance guidance, website clicks count clicks on the profile's website link. For hotels, booking clicks also count clicks on a booking link, not completed reservations. Check which metrics are available. Use the hotel's own records for bookings after accounting for cancellations, and for actual stays.

Our café marketing article similarly separates saves from actual visits. Hotels also need to distinguish signs of interest from room sales to decide where explanations or replies need improvement.

Frequently asked questions

Is listing on Agoda and Trip.com enough for hotel marketing?

A listing creates a sales channel. Also maintain the Business Profile and website where someone searching the hotel name can verify location and official details. Platform priorities depend on target countries, room types and the current booking mix.

Should the official website always be cheaper than Agoda or Trip.com?

There is no single answer. Check platform contracts, tax, included benefits and refund terms first. Rather than focusing on a different displayed price, check whether guests can compare rooms, benefits and cancellation conditions on the same basis.

How should hotel photographs be prepared?

Show current rooms and facilities accurately, and keep images assigned to the correct room type. When comparing results before and after changes, consider other influences such as day of the week, peak seasons and room prices.
