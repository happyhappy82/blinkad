What Google Business Profile Helps You Manage

Google Business Profile is free for eligible businesses that meet customers in person. Authorized owners can maintain information associated with Search and Maps so that people can understand the business and plan their next step. Local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence; setup does not purchase a position.

Get the Everyday Details Right

1. Make the Core Information Dependable

  • Name, location, phone and hours: use the recognized real-world name, actual eligible address or service area, working contact number and current hours. The source placed businesses on Hannuri-daero (34.8%), Namsejong-ro (25.9%) and Horyeoul-ro (19.2%). Those shares do not establish foot traffic or search demand; provide your own location and entrance accurately.
  • Website and booking links: send people to relevant, working pages. Use supported booking or ordering fields or providers where available, and check the destination and terms. A button click does not show that a reservation or order was completed.

2. Choose Categories That Describe the Business

  • Primary category: select the most accurate available option for the main business. The source listed Korean food at 118 businesses, other casual food services at 96 and other education at 82. A larger count does not justify a misleading category or establish that a particular search is difficult.
  • Additional categories: Google’s current editing guide allows up to nine, but use the fewest relevant categories. “Boram-dong fusion Korean food” or “Boram-dong home-style set meals” may suggest a factual menu explanation; they are not custom categories you can invent, and a narrower phrase does not prove lower competition.

3. Show What a First-Time Visitor Needs to See

  • Exterior and entrance: show the actual approach and floor. The source gave ground-floor businesses at 21.3%, floors two to five at 19.5%, and the sixth floor or above at 8.6%. These partial figures do not establish accessibility. Explain your actual stairs, lift or entrance conditions accurately.
  • Interior: show representative views of the real space rather than a setting the customer will not encounter.
  • Menu or products: use images of what is actually available and keep any accompanying information or conditions current.
  • People: obtain appropriate permissions for staff or customer images and show genuine service or work examples.

4. Leave Reviews to Genuine Customer Experiences

  • Reply to the specific feedback: check the facts, acknowledge an actual concern appropriately and protect private information. Describe completed improvements only when true, without an automatic admission or a promise about a future visit.
  • Make optional feedback easy: provide a neutral review link or QR code for genuine customers. Keep any event or offer separate from review requests. Do not give rewards, select only happy customers, apply pressure or request a rating or wording. Review quantity is not a promise of a fixed Google score or ranking.

5. Share News with Accurate Dates and Terms

  • New menus and products: explain an actual addition with a relevant permitted photo and correct availability.
  • Events and offers: use supported post types and genuine conditions, including the applicable dates. Check publication status rather than assuming that every submission is immediately visible.
  • Hours changes: explain useful context in a post where appropriate, and update the hours field as well. A post about a closure should not leave the main hours incorrect.

Decide What Needs More Explanation

Explain the Actual Service in Larger Categories

The source listed Korean food at 118 businesses, other casual food services at 96, other education at 82, real-estate services at 76 and general education at 61. Its “red ocean” label does not measure competition for your exact service. Explain actual menus, courses, service scope and relevant qualifications, using representative photos and helpful contact information.

Smaller Counts Do Not Establish an Easy Opening

Pet and pet-supplies retail, furniture retail, household-goods rental, watch and jewellery retail, and other-goods retail each had three businesses in the source. This does not establish unmet demand or a first-mover advantage. Review actual customer needs and service capacity before treating a small count as an expansion opportunity.

Use the Correct Building and Entrance

Horyeoul Village Complex 7 (94 businesses), Naver Tower (58) and Gangnam Jeil Tower (55) were original examples. They are not verified current clients or addresses. Mention a building or floor when it accurately helps a customer find the premises, rather than adding it to the business name for search coverage.

Assign Responsibility for Changes

Decide who will check changed hours, services, contact routes and incoming feedback. Keep useful news current and correct inaccurate photos. Frequency alone does not establish a ranking bonus or a requirement to keep inventing events.
