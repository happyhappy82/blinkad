DoctorNest brings inquiries and conversation history from connected channels together. The aim is to keep earlier answers and the next colleague's tasks alongside the messages, so the team can continue the conversation.

Discuss inquiry management for your clinic

Begin with the channels your team uses most

Think about where your clinic's inquiries arrive. If staff move between KakaoTalk and Instagram DMs, look at where they spend time searching for earlier answers. You do not need to replace every channel or create an entirely new process at the outset.

Viewing inquiries from connected channels together in DoctorNest can reduce the need to reopen each app to find a conversation. Before replying, read that inquiry's earlier messages and check what the patient has already been told.

Whether an existing account can be connected depends on the channel and account type. Tell us which channels you use during the adoption discussion, and we can explain current connection options and how staff would view them on a PC.

Record what the next colleague needs to do

A saved conversation is less helpful if the next person has to read every long message from the beginning. Briefly note which questions have been answered, what still needs checking and when someone has agreed to contact the patient again.

For example, if a patient has sent a preferred date, record whether the booking team has checked actual availability. Clearly distinguish a requested date from a confirmed appointment in the notes, so the next staff member can give accurate information.

Use DoctorNest's conversation history and notes to carry those details forward. Confirm appointment status and clinical information in the clinic's existing booking system and medical record system. Keep the inquiry note focused on what staff need to do next.

Save recurring information, then let staff take over

Confirmed information, such as the clinic's location and opening hours, can be prepared as saved replies. Begin with wording your team uses frequently, so staff do not have to type the same answer from scratch each time.

Individual prices, suitability for a procedure and available appointment times still need the responsible person's confirmation. Rather than rush to give a definite answer, explain what needs to be checked and when you will get back to the patient.

When opening hours or promotion conditions change, review the saved replies as well. Assigning someone to maintain the wording helps prevent old information from being sent. If you are considering DoctorNest, start by showing us examples of the answers your team regularly uses.
