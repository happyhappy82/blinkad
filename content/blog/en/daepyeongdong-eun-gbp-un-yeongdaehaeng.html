Frequently Asked Questions

Why consider profile management in Daepyeong-dong?

How often should the information be updated?

Correct essential facts when they change, especially hours, location and contact routes. Add worthwhile news or photos when relevant. Weekly posting is an editorial suggestion, not proof of an automatic ranking bonus or a reason to invent promotions.

How can customers be invited to leave a review?

Make optional, genuine feedback easy with a neutral invitation and a working review link or QR code. Keep it separate from rewards or offers, do not select only happy customers, and leave the content and rating to the customer. A request does not guarantee a response or a higher score.
