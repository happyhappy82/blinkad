Read the Local Figures Before Choosing the Work

Google Business Profile is free for eligible businesses that meet customers in person. Authorized owners can maintain information associated with Search and Maps. Local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence.

Prepare the Details Customers Will Use

1. Keep the Core Information Accurate

  • Name: use the recognized real-world business name without adding location or service terms solely for SEO.
  • Address: provide the actual eligible location or appropriate service area, with useful entrance and floor details where needed.
  • Phone: keep a working number that reaches the business.
  • Hours: maintain regular hours and appropriate special-hours fields for holidays or changes, then check the public result.
  • Website: link to a relevant working page and supported booking route where available. A click does not establish a completed reservation or enquiry.
  • Categories: choose an accurate available primary category and only relevant additional ones. Ribs, stew or a particular meal can belong in menu content; they are not automatically available categories.

2. Help a First-Time Customer Recognize the Place

  • Photos: use representative views of the real entrance, space, menu, products or service, with appropriate permissions. A ground-floor classification does not establish accessibility; explain actual stairs, lifts or entrance conditions where relevant.
  • Video: a relevant short introduction may help someone understand the setting. Use permitted, supported content rather than treating a video as evidence of future visits.

3. Keep Hours and News Current

  • Posts: use supported features for actual news, events, menus or permitted offers with current dates and terms, and check publication status. Hotel profiles cannot publish offer posts or posts mentioning or linking to deals, promotions or discounts. Follow the policies applicable to the actual category.
  • Special hours: update the relevant field for holiday or temporary changes. A post explaining a closure should not leave the main hours wrong. Posting frequency alone does not establish a ranking bonus.

4. Keep Reviews Genuine and Independent

  • Invite honest feedback: offer genuine customers a neutral, optional review link or QR code. Do not select only happy customers or give free drinks, gifts, discounts or other rewards for posting, changing or removing a review. Avoid pressure and requested wording.
  • Reply to the actual feedback: check the facts, protect private information and describe supported actions or improvements. Apologize appropriately when the facts support it, without automatically promising a better future visit.
  • Read ratings in context: the original article’s suggestion that a 0.1-point difference can have a large ranking effect is not established here as a fixed rule. Genuine reviews and ratings can contribute to prominence alongside other factors; no score or reply routine guarantees a position.

5. Explain the Real Service in Ordinary Language

  • Description: phrases such as “restaurant in Geonip-dong,” “Geonip-dong salon” or “café on Imhang-ro” can help plan an explanation if the service and location are accurate. These are English illustrations, not newly measured Korean queries. Avoid repetition or business-name stuffing.
  • Services and menus: describe actual cuts, perms, colouring, dishes or other offerings through supported fields, with accurate conditions where shown. The source’s examples are not evidence of a particular business’s current service list.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the business has very few reviews?

Make genuine optional feedback easy with a neutral invitation and a working link or QR code. Leave the rating and wording to the customer. Rewards, including a small gift or discount, should not be exchanged for a review or a change to one. A request does not guarantee a response, positive rating or customer growth.

How long does it take to rank higher?

There is no reliable deadline from this source. Its expectation of improvement over weeks or months is not a guarantee, and the larger or smaller category labels do not establish a timetable. First distinguish access, verification and factual corrections from the profile’s position for a specific query. Assess the actual search context and completed enquiries separately from profile actions.
