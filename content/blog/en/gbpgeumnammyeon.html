What a Useful Profile Can Help With

Google Business Profile is free for eligible businesses that meet customers in person. Authorized owners can maintain information associated with Search and Maps. Local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and no one can purchase a better organic local position.

  • Local-business context: the source classified 94.7% of the 663 businesses as local. That share does not establish a preference for independent shops or measure how often residents search on Google.
  • Larger source categories: Korean food accounted for 131 businesses, real-estate services for 54 and non-alcoholic beverage services for 45. Explain actual service differences rather than treating these counts as a score for ranking difficulty.

Get the Everyday Details Right

1. Keep the Core Facts Accurate

  • Business name: use the recognized real-world name, without adding an area or service solely for SEO.
  • Address: provide the actual eligible location or appropriate service area. Write Sejong City once in the source’s illustrative 68 Yongpo-ro address and confirm the actual premises and map position before use.
  • Phone: keep a working contact number that reaches the business.
  • Hours: maintain regular hours, relevant breaks or closures and applicable special hours, then check the public result.
  • Website: link to a relevant working page where a customer can understand the service and make contact.

2. Choose Categories and Explain the Actual Service

  • Primary category: select the most accurate available option for the main business.
  • Additional categories: use only relevant available options. A lunch buffet may warrant an additional category if it accurately describes the business; a menu item or meal occasion does not automatically qualify.
  • Products and services: explain what is actually offered through supported fields, using current menu images, descriptions, prices and conditions where relevant.

3. Show the Place and Its Offering

  • Interior and exterior: show representative views of the actual space and entrance.
  • Menu or products: use images of items genuinely offered and keep accompanying information current.
  • Service activity: obtain appropriate permissions for identifiable people and customer material, and use genuine work examples.
  • Video: a relevant short introduction may help explain the setting or service. Use supported formats and content, rather than treating video as evidence of future visits.

4. Keep Feedback Independent and Replies Factual

Reviews can contribute to prominence and help prospective customers understand genuine experiences. The public ranking guidance does not establish reviews as a universally largest factor or promise a position from a reply routine.

  • Invite honest feedback: offer actual customers a neutral, optional review route. Do not select only satisfied customers, give incentives, apply pressure or request particular words or ratings.
  • Reply appropriately: check the issue, protect private information and explain only supported facts or completed improvements. An apology should fit the actual circumstances, rather than become an automatic admission or a future-result promise.
  • Follow up on the issue: use an appropriate private contact route when needed and available. Keep the focus on resolving the actual concern, without rewards or pressure to change or remove a review.

5. Share Useful News with Real Conditions

  • Menus, events and offers: publish actual updates through supported features, with accurate dates, availability and terms. Check publication status.
  • Hours changes: explain useful context in a post where appropriate and update the hours field as well.
  • Service-specific news: a restaurant or beverage business may have real seasonal menus or takeaway offers; an eligible property agency may have current, authorized listing information or sourced market updates. Confirm facts, dates, permissions and any professional scope before publishing.
