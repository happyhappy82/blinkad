Get the Customer Information Right First

1. Start with the Service a Customer Is Looking For

  • More detailed questions: a particular dish or price condition can help organize an explanation when actually offered. A longer phrase does not automatically have low volume, clear purchase intent or lower competition.
  • Smaller source categories: medicine/cosmetics retail and travel agency/support services each had three businesses. That does not establish unmet demand or eligibility to describe any retailer as a pharmacy. Use the actual professional scope and qualifications.
  • Actual location: the source placed 108 businesses on Beonyeong-ro and 52 on Myeongnim-ro. Mention a road only when accurate; these counts do not establish foot traffic or popular search phrases.

2. Get the Core Details Right

  • Name, address and contact: use the recognized real-world name, actual eligible location or service area, working phone, current hours and relevant website. The source’s 500 Beonyeong-ro example needs confirmation before use.
  • Categories: choose an accurate available primary category and only relevant additional ones. “Jeju local restaurant” is not a custom category to invent when the actual available category differs.
  • Photos and video: show representative, permitted views of the entrance, space and actual service. Ten photos can be a planning target, not a ranking minimum. The source’s 39.0% ground-floor share does not prove accessibility for a particular business.
  • Description: explain actual service details in ordinary language. The source’s 200–300-character idea is a drafting suggestion, while Google’s description limit is 750 characters. Avoid repeated keywords, links, sales promotions or unsupported claims such as being the only charcoal-grilled black-pork restaurant.

4. Assign Responsibility for Ongoing Changes

  • Reviews: invite genuine customers neutrally and leave participation optional, without incentives, positive-only selection, pressure or requested wording. Reply to the actual facts, protect private information and describe supported checks or improvements rather than promising a better future visit.
  • Posts: share real news, menus, events or permitted offers through supported features, with current dates and conditions. Check publication status and update changed hours in their own field as well.
  • Common questions: answer actual parking or booking questions through the website or currently supported profile features. The former Q&A API ended on November 3, 2025; that does not establish identical question features on every profile or a guaranteed search advantage.
  • Maintenance: a monthly check can be a reminder, but correct essential changes when they occur. The interval is not a Google ranking requirement or an agreed workload.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it differ from Naver SmartPlace?

They serve different search and map platforms, with separate management arrangements. Choose priorities from actual customer discovery and contact needs; the source’s local-business share does not establish which platform is best or a transfer of ranking benefits between them.

How can a business explain a real difference?

Describe actual dishes, services, facilities or qualifications that help a customer decide. Substantiate an exclusivity claim before using it, and use permitted representative photos. A longer search phrase or a smaller source category is not proof of an uncontested position.

How should negative reviews be handled?

Read the specific concern, check the facts and reply appropriately, keeping private details private. Apologize when the facts support it and explain completed actions truthfully. A standard promise about a future visit does not resolve every issue or guarantee trust, a changed review or business growth.
